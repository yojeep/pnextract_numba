Throat Parameters

1. Throat Center and Radius

The throat center is defined as the center of the medial ball associated with the throat:

xt=(tr.mb22()->fk, tr.mb22()->fj, tr.mb22()->fi)\mathbf{x}_t = \big( \texttt{tr.mb22()->fk},\ \texttt{tr.mb22()->fj},\ \texttt{tr.mb22()->fi} \big)xt​=(tr.mb22()->fk, tr.mb22()->fj, tr.mb22()->fi)

This point lies on the medial axis and represents the geometric “skeleton” location of the narrowest passage between two pores.

The throat radius is bounded by the medial ball at the throat and the radii of its two adjacent pores:

Rt=min⁡(max⁡(Rtmb, 0.5), Rp1, Rp2)+δR_t = \min\left( \max(R_t^{\text{mb}},\, 0.5),\, R_{p_1},\, R_{p_2} \right) + \deltaRt​=min(max(Rtmb​,0.5),Rp1​​,Rp2​​)+δ

  • RtmbR_t^{\text{mb}}Rtmb​: radius of the maximal inscribed sphere (medial ball) at the throat
  • δ∼U(−0.25, +0.25)\delta \sim \mathcal{U}(-0.25,\, +0.25)δ∼U(−0.25,+0.25): small random perturbation for numerical stability
    • Implemented as:
      δ=0.5×(0.5−rand()RAND_MAX)\delta = 0.5 \times \left(0.5 - \frac{\texttt{rand()}}{\texttt{RAND\_MAX}}\right)δ=0.5×(0.5−RAND_MAXrand()​)

2. Cross-Sectional Vector Ct\mathbf{C}_tCt​ and Area AtA_tAt​

During network construction (createNewThroats), for every pair of adjacent voxels belonging to different pores p1p_1p1​ and p2p_2p2​, the code detects a face interface and accumulates a signed count into the throat’s CrosArea vector:

  • If the interface is perpendicular to the x-axis, then:
    Cx←Cx+(2⋅I[p2>p1]−1)C_x \leftarrow C_x + (2 \cdot \mathbb{I}[p_2 > p_1] - 1)Cx​←Cx​+(2⋅I[p2​>p1​]−1)
  • Similarly for y-axis → CyC_yCy​, and z-axis → CzC_zCz​.

Here, I[⋅]\mathbb{I}[\cdot]I[⋅] is the indicator function, and the sign ensures symmetry: swapping p1p_1p1​ and p2p_2p2​ flips the sign of Ct\mathbf{C}_tCt​, but At=∥Ct∥A_t = \|\mathbf{C}_t\|At​=∥Ct​∥ remains unchanged.

The effective cross-sectional area is then:

At=∥Ct∥=Cx2+Cy2+Cz2A_t = \|\mathbf{C}_t\| = \sqrt{C_x^2 + C_y^2 + C_z^2}At​=∥Ct​∥=Cx2​+Cy2​+Cz2​​

Note: AtA_tAt​ is dimensionless and proportional to the number of voxel faces connecting the two pores. It serves as a discrete proxy for hydraulic conductance area.

3. Pore Volume Adjustment

An effective cross-sectional area for the pore is estimated as:

Ap=Rp24GpA_p = \frac{R_p^2}{4 G_p}Ap​=4Gp​Rp2​​

Total weighting area:

Atotal=∑t∈T(p)At+ApA_{\text{total}} = \sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}(p)} A_t + A_pAtotal​=t∈T(p)∑​At​+Ap​

Let VporigV_p^{\text{orig}}Vporig​ be the original geometric volume of pore ppp (counted during voxel labeling). Then:

  • Adjusted pore volume:

    Vp=Vporig⋅ApAtotalV_p = V_p^{\text{orig}} \cdot \frac{A_p}{A_{\text{total}}}Vp​=Vporig​⋅Atotal​Ap​​

  • Each connected throat ttt receives a volume share:

    Vt+=Vporig⋅AtAtotalV_t \mathrel{+}= V_p^{\text{orig}} \cdot \frac{A_t}{A_{\text{total}}}Vt​+=Vporig​⋅Atotal​At​​

This ensures total volume conservation while redistributing pore volume based on hydraulic relevance.
